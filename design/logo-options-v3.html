Logo options, round 3

Fewer ideas, more depth: three takes on Just the Video, three on today’s viewfinder, and two mixes of both, with Current, Held and Soft Switch kept for comparison. Switch the palette in the sidebar to redraw every concept, light and dark. All 16px drawings are hand-tuned and use one flat red.

Designer’s pick

Framed Player

I recommend Framed Player. It carries forward the one thing people already recognise, the viewfinder corners, but swaps the play triangle for YouTube’s own progress bar, which takes it well away from YouTube’s trade dress. At 16px it stays crisp (four 2px corners around a bright knob) and it holds up in all three palettes; it is at its best in Red to Magenta, where the bar’s gradient becomes visible. If testing shows it reading as a screen recorder, Wide Screen is my runner-up: the same idea without the corners, and the clearest “Just the Video”.

Mockups marked light or dark keep their colours whatever the page theme. All text meets WCAG AA. Every tinted colour stays within 25° of YouTube red’s hue (348°); the YouTube Red palette uses neutral greys, and the mockups use Chrome’s own UI colours.
