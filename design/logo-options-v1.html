Designer’s pick

Held

I recommend Held. It is the only option that keeps a thread existing users will recognise (something framing a play mark) while fixing what feels off today: the camera corners become open, cradling parentheses, and alarm red becomes calm periwinkle on ink. At 16px it still reads clearly as a play mark between two brackets on both light and dark toolbars, is plainly not YouTube’s red-box-and-play, and fits any of the three candidate names. If you want a bolder break, Quiet Room is my runner-up: the most emotional and ownable mark, best paired with “Quiet Mode for YouTube”.

Mockups labelled light or dark keep their colours regardless of the page theme. Contrast ratios are WCAG 2.x; text in every mockup meets AA.
