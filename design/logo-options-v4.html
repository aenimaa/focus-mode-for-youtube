Logo options, round 4

Fewer, better variants, and one rule: the 16px and 32px icons are the same picture as the 128px logo. Each master is its 16px drawing scaled up with lighter strokes, and where an element could not survive 16px, it was removed from the big logo too. Wine is the new default palette.

Designer’s pick

Framed Player A, in Wine

I recommend Framed Player A in the Wine palette. It is now the same picture at every size, frame, screen and progress bar, so the toolbar icon finally matches the store logo. The corners carry the “focus” that people already know from today’s icon, and the dark screen with YouTube’s bar inside says “video” without a play triangle on a red box. Wine keeps round 3’s depth while the ink screen stands out clearly on both tiles. If A feels too busy at 16px, Framed Player B is my runner-up: the same idea with fewer parts.

Mockups marked light or dark keep their own colours. All text meets WCAG AA. Every tinted colour stays within 20° of YouTube red’s hue (348°), and Wine within 12°. YouTube Red uses neutral greys, and the mockups use Chrome’s own UI colours.
