Logo options, round 5 (final)

The last round: Held in three versions, the merged Framed Player, and three round-4 concepts kept for reference, all in Wine. The rule from round 4 still holds: the 16px and 32px icons are the same picture as the 128px logo, and only stroke weights change between sizes.

Designer’s pick

Squircle Held, named Focus Mode

I recommend Squircle Held. Held says the brand best: the one video you chose, held gently while everything else steps aside. That feels kind and on your side, not like a lock or a blocker. The squircle version is the strongest of the three. Its straight runs land on whole pixels, so at 16px the brackets read as two crisp red strokes, and the softened square corners sit naturally next to Chrome’s other toolbar icons. The play mark is ink inside a frame, never white on a red box. If you want the icon to say “video” more literally, Framed Player is my runner-up: the merged version is calmer than A, and its red stripe is a detail every YouTube viewer already knows.

Mockups marked light or dark keep their own colours. All text meets WCAG AA. Every Wine colour stays within 12° of YouTube red’s hue (348°), and the mockups use Chrome’s own UI colours.
