Logo options, round 2

One family in YouTube’s red-to-magenta: every concept sits on a blush (light) or wine (dark) tile with a soft top shine, and the gradient lives in the glyph, never as a red box behind a white play. 16px drawings are hand-tuned and flattened to one solid red so nothing turns to mud. Pick a concept here or in the sidebar.

Designer’s pick

Just the Video

I recommend Just the Video. It’s the one concept where YouTube’s red-to-magenta gradient carries meaning: it is the progress line, so the icon sits next to YouTube like a native companion without borrowing its red box and play button. At 16px it is still two clear shapes on both toolbars, a calm block over a bright bar with a knob, and it pairs naturally with “Just the Video”, a name the check did not flag. If you’d rather keep a thread to today’s framed-play icon, Held is my runner-up, and it suits “Tucked” well.

Mockups marked light or dark keep their colours whatever the page theme. All text meets WCAG AA. Every colour stays within 20° of YouTube red’s hue (348°), apart from Chrome’s own UI greys and blues in the mockups.
