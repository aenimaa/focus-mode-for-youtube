Focus Mode for YouTube

Watch the video you came for. The rest stays tucked away.

A free extension for Chrome and Firefox · open source · no tracking · not affiliated with YouTube

⬇️ Download for Chrome 🦊 Get it for Firefox View on GitHub

🚀 Install in a minute

🦊 On Firefox? Add it from Firefox Add-ons in one click, and it updates by itself. The steps below are for Chrome and other Chromium browsers.

1Unzip the download

You'll get one folder.

2Turn on Developer mode

Open chrome://extensions; the switch is at the top right.

3Load unpacked

Choose the unzipped folder. That's it.

📌 Pin it from the puzzle-piece icon in Chrome's toolbar.

🙈 What it tucks away

Each part has its own switch, and you can bring any of them back at any time.

On the video page

🎞️ Related videos

The "Up next" column beside the player. The video moves to the center; playlists, live chat and transcripts stay.

🎬 End screen

The cards and suggestion grid that appear as a video ends. Autoplay's countdown stays visible.

💬 Comments

The comments under the video.

📝 Description

The box under the title with views, date and description.

On Home

📱 Shorts

Rows of Shorts, single Shorts in search and under videos, and the Shorts menu entry.

🧭 Explore

The Explore section of the left menu, and Home's "Explore more topics" row.

🎛️ Controls

⏸️ Pause when you want YouTube as usual

Master has three settings: On, Pause and Off. Pause for 15 minutes, 1 hour or until tomorrow morning, and it comes back by itself.

🫧 A quiet corner badge

A small badge in the bottom-left corner of YouTube shows what's tucked away. It stays dim until you hover it and shows itself once a day. Close it and you get a few seconds to undo, or switch it off in the popup.

🗺️ A popup with a map

The Focus tab shows a small map of the video page and of Home. Tap a tile, or the area itself, to tuck it away or bring it back. Settings and About are one tab away.

🔒 Safe by design

It runs only on youtube.com, its only permission is saving its own settings, and it makes no network requests. Nothing is tracked or collected.

How much access does an extension need?

More access isn't bad by itself: a tool that works on every website has to ask for every website. It just means more trust. Here's where common kinds of extensions sit.

Works on any website you visit Every website (ad blockers, translators, dark-mode tools)

📚 More

🔄 Updating to a new version

Download the new zip, unzip it over your old folder, and click Reload on the extension's card in chrome://extensions. Your settings stay, even if you move or rename the folder.

🌐 Other browsers

🦊 Firefox 140+: install it from Firefox Add-ons. It updates by itself.

Works in Chrome 107+ and other Chromium browsers: Edge, Brave, Opera and Vivaldi. Their extensions page has the same Developer mode and Load unpacked options.

⚙️ How it works

The hiding rules are plain CSS, applied before YouTube draws the page, so nothing flashes on screen first. A tiny script switches rules off for the parts you've chosen to keep. Changes apply to every open YouTube tab at once, and with Chrome sync or Firefox Sync on, your choices follow you to your other computers.

extension/      The extension: what's in the download
  hide.css      Rules that tuck each part away
