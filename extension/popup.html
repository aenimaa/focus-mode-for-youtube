No Distractions for YouTube

Control what stays on screen

Master

  • Hides the "Up next" column beside the player. Playlists, live chat and transcripts stay.

  • Hides the comment section below the video.

  • Hides the rows of Shorts on Home, Search and under videos.

  • Hides the box under the title with views, date and description.

Appearance
  • Runs only on youtube.com
  • Its only permission is saving these settings
  • No network requests, tracking or data collection
  • Only hides parts of the page; never reads or changes your account
